Our story

Born from a relentless pursuit of clean living

Provenance and purity, no compromise. ALFATAH Market began with a simple frustration: reading the back of a label should not be detective work.

We stock day-to-day essentials that help you establish an achievable daily wellbeing practice — pantry staples, household supplies and personal care, each vetted for provenance and ingredients before it reaches a shelf.

Everything ships from our Doha store across Qatar.
